How rewards work

Two ways a round can end

01

Knockout

The crown window expires before the hard clock. The champion receives 40% and crown-time holders split 50%.

02

Decision

Challenges extend the crown window to the hard clock. Crown-time holders split 90%; there is no champion reward.

03

Rollover

The remaining 10% starts the next round’s pot. Every ordinary pool swap also adds its game fee.
